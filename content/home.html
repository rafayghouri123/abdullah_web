F.A.Q

Straightforward answers, so you can move forward with confidence.
What does MOBIN IMPEX do?
MOBIN IMPEX is a licensed customs clearing and forwarding agent offering customs clearance, freight forwarding and ancillary logistics services.
When was MOBIN IMPEX established?
The company was established and obtained its customs license in 1996.
What shipping methods do you offer?
We offer sea freight (FCL/LCL), air freight, local and regional road transport, and rail logistics.
What is included in customs clearance?
Import and export documentation, duty and tax assessment, HS code classification, liaison with customs authorities, temporary import/export permits, and port health and quarantine formalities.
Do you handle project cargo and dangerous goods?
Yes. Project cargo handling and dangerous goods transport are part of our freight forwarding services.
What additional cargo services are available?
Warehousing and storage, cargo insurance, packaging and labeling, door-to-door delivery, and tracking and reporting.
Can I track my cargo or get support?
We provide real-time cargo tracking and 24/7 customer support.
